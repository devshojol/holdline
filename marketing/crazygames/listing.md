# Sunstrike: store listing

Copy for the CrazyGames submission form. Only the title appears on the covers.

**Title:** Sunstrike

**Category:** Shooting · **Tags:** Multiplayer, FPS, Gun, 3D, PvP

**Short description**

Fast online FPS in sunlit streets. Jump into a five-minute deathmatch in seconds.

**Description**

Sunstrike is a fast online first-person shooter set in sunny old towns, desert bazaars, island forts and snowy ports. Hit Quick Play and you drop straight into a five-minute deathmatch against up to nine other players. There is always a fight waiting.

- Eight hand-built maps, each with its own look: white-walled plazas, a desert bazaar, a hillside temple, neon back streets, jungle ruins, an arctic port, an island fort and a sunlit old town.
- Eight guns, from rifles and an SMG to a scoped sniper and three sidearms, each with its own recoil.
- Ten soldiers to play as.
- Play with friends: make a room, send the link, set the map and the rules.
- Footsteps show on the minimap, a full map is one key away, and wall signs point the way to each site.
- Practice against bots on any map: hold an angle, clear a site, defuse a charge or train your recoil.
- Every key can be changed in the settings.

Most kills when the five minutes run out wins.

**Controls**

- WASD: move
- Mouse: aim, left click to shoot
- Right click: aim down sights / scope
- R: reload
- Shift: walk quietly
- C: crouch
- Space: jump
- 1 / 2 or mouse wheel: switch weapon
- B: weapon menu
- M: map · hold Tab: map and scoreboard
- Esc: pause
